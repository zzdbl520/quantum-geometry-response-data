is given in the main text, SI, and this release's README.

| Claim | Primary distributed record | Verification |
|---|---|---|

| Theory: support restriction, covariance, split-cluster response, and cusp | `scripts/verify_revision.py` | Independent finite-dimensional calculations with exact diagonalization and a fixed random seed. |
